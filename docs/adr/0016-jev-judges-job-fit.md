# Jev judges job fit; code blends it into the Tradie ranking

Recommended Tradies used to be ranked by strict tie-breaks: Nominated, then myEST8 rating, then Google rating, then median Firm Quote. Two things were wrong with that. Nothing looked at the Job itself, so a plumber who had replaced ten hot water systems ranked the same as one who had only cleared drains. And because a myEST8 average is a decimal that rarely ties exactly, the Google rating and the price almost never counted.

**Jev scores job fit.** For each Tradie with the Job's Trade, myEST8 asks Jev (TypeSafe's System One model) one Score question about how closely the Tradie's record matches the repair. The levels are no related work, Trade only, related work, and this same kind of job. Jev is shown the Job's Trade, Summary, description and location, plus the Tradie's latest ten confirmed Quotes on other Jobs (each Job's summary and the Firm Quote's scope of work) and what their Google listing says they do. Contact details are removed first. Each Tradie is a separate request, as in TypeSafe's re-ranking cookbook, so no judgment sees another Tradie and the scores mean the same thing however many are compared. `src/adapters/jev-job-fit.ts` holds the question; `JEV_API_URL` and `JEV_API_KEY` configure it, as for classification ([ADR 0011](0011-jev-classifies-haiku-composes.md)).

**Pages never wait on Jev.** Judgments are kept in `job_fits` with a hash of the evidence Jev was shown. When a recommendation is shown, any Tradie without a current judgment schedules one `quotes.judge_fit` task, and the list is ranked without job fit until it lands. A new Summary, a new Quote on another Job, or a newly added Tradie changes the evidence and so asks again. A failed judgment retries like any task and never blocks sending Quote Requests.

**Code owns the weights.** After the Nominated Tradies, recommendations sort by a weighted mean: myEST8 rating 0.4, Google rating 0.2, job fit 0.3 and price 0.1 (the cheapest median Firm Quote over the Tradie's own), each scaled to 0–1 (`RANK_WEIGHTS` in `src/quotes.ts`). A part a Tradie has no value for is left out and the others share its weight. So a Tradie who isn't on Google is ranked on the starting rating the Property Manager gave them, as the design asks, rather than being marked down. The parts are still shown in their own columns, never as one combined number.

We chose a blend over putting job fit straight after Nominated, because a strict key would let one AI judgment outrank every rating. We chose it over showing job fit without ranking by it, because then the ranking would keep ignoring the Job. Dispatch keeps its own nearest-first order, since its job is finding someone close by.
