# Property Managers sign in through WorkOS AuthKit

Property Managers sign in and sign up on one page: WorkOS AuthKit's hosted page. `/login` sends the browser there, `/signup` opens it on creating an account, and AuthKit comes back to `/callback`. We chose this over keeping our own sign-in and sign-up forms because AuthKit gives password resets, email verification, Google sign-in and invitation emails without us building or securing them, on a free tier.

AuthKit only says who someone is. myEST8 keeps its own `sessions` table and its own `property_managers`, matched to AuthKit by verified email, so nothing else in the app changed and no AuthKit user id is stored. Someone AuthKit signs in with no Property Manager is new: `/signup` asks them for their name and their Agency, then they become its first Property Manager, as before. A Property Manager adding a colleague, in the setup guide or in Settings, creates their Property Manager straight away and WorkOS emails them an invitation. If the invitation fails to send, signing up through AuthKit with that email still signs them in, because the email already has a Property Manager.

The cost is that sign-in now depends on WorkOS being up and configured: each environment needs WORKOS_API_KEY, WORKOS_CLIENT_ID and WORKOS_COOKIE_PASSWORD, and its WorkOS environment needs `PUBLIC_URL/callback` as a Redirect URI, `PUBLIC_URL/` as the Sign-out URI and `PUBLIC_URL/login` as the Initiate login URI. Without those settings, and always for the seeded demo Agency, whose made-up Property Managers sign in from the landing page, the old email and password forms stay. Accounts made with a password before this keep working: AuthKit signs them in by the same email.
